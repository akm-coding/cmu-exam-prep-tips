# CMU International Program Admission (IPAS) 2027

## Overview

Chiang Mai University (CMU) accepts applications for international undergraduate programs through the CMU International Program Admission System (CMU-IPAS) at https://admission.reg.cmu.ac.th/ipas/. Nineteen programs are offered for the 2027 academic year, including Software Engineering (SE) and Modern Management and Information Technology (MMIT) at the College of Arts, Media and Technology (CAMT).

Applicants may apply for up to five (5) programs per admission round, but may accept only one (1) offer. They can log in during the application period to add programs (up to the 5-program limit) even after paying for earlier submissions.

## Programs offered

- Faculty of Humanities: Humanities and Sustainability (HS), Thai as a Foreign Language (TFL)
- Faculty of Social Sciences: Social Science and Sustainable Development (SSSD)
- Faculty of Science: Environmental Science (ES)
- Faculty of Engineering: Civil and Infrastructure Engineering (CIE), Electrical Engineering and Smart Grid Technology (EESG), Industrial Engineering and Logistics Management (IEL), Information Systems and Cybersecurity (ISCE), Integrated and Multi-disciplinary Engineering (IGME), Mechanical Engineering and Engineering Project Management (MEPM)
- Faculty of Nursing: Nursing Science (NSC)
- Faculty of Agro-Industry: Food Science and Technology (FST)
- Faculty of Business Administration: Accountancy (ACC)
- Faculty of Economics: Economics (ECON)
- Faculty of Architecture: Integrated Design in Emerging Architecture (IDEA)
- Faculty of Political Sciences and Public Administration: Sustainability (STB)
- College of Arts, Media and Technology: Modern Management and Information Technology (MMIT), Software Engineering (SE)
- International College of Digital Innovation: Digital Innovation (DIN)

Software Engineering (SE) is open in Round 1 and Round 2 only (not Round 3). Round 3 is limited to Thai applicants and international applicants holding a valid Non-Immigrant ED Visa (or ED Plus Visa) from a recognized Thai educational institution.

Applicants with a Thai National ID or a 13-digit DMC ID (beginning with G-code, 0, 00, 6, or 7) must confirm acceptance in both TCAS (https://student.mytcas.com/) and CMU-IPAS within the stated period. Failing to confirm in either system counts as rejecting the offer.

## Exam day schedule (Regular Track)

On the written examination day of each round:

- 09:00 – 12:00: Subject test(s) (CMU EXAM). Applicants who need more than one specialized subject exam (for example SE applicants take Mathematics **and** the Software Engineering Aptitude Test) must complete all of them in this same 09:00 – 12:00 window.
- 13:00 – 16:00: English Placement Test (CMU EPT), for applicants without a qualifying English score.
- Interviews follow over the next several days, 09:00 – 16:00, online.

CMU EPT results are valid only for the round in which the test is taken and cannot be carried to later rounds.

## Regular Track vs Special Track

- **Regular Track**: for applicants who do not meet the English proficiency requirement and/or do not have the required standardized test scores. They take the CMU EPT and/or the program's specialized subject exams (CMU EXAM), then an online interview.
- **Special Track**: for applicants who already meet the English requirement and hold the standardized scores the program requires. No CMU EXAM — only an online interview.

Flow: register in IPAS → select program(s) and track (up to 5) → upload documents (including test results) → pay fee → Regular: CMU EXAM, or Special: no exam → interview.

## English proficiency rules

- Native English speakers (citizens of Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, UK, USA) are exempt.
- Non-native speakers may be exempt if they are citizens of a country where English is an official language (Annex 2 list, e.g. India, Singapore, Philippines, Pakistan, Nigeria, Kenya, Hong Kong), or graduated from an international program / English-medium institution.
- **Important:** some programs, including SE, MMIT, ECON and HS, do **not** exempt applicants who graduated from an international program or English-medium school — those applicants must still submit a qualifying score or take the CMU EPT.
- Applicants without a valid qualifying score take the CMU English Placement Test (EPT).

## Required documents

Each uploaded file must be 1 MB or smaller; non-Thai/English documents need an official English translation.

1. Official graduation certificate (or certificate of current student status if not yet graduated)
2. Official academic transcript
3. English proficiency test result (if required for the track/program)
4. Standardized test results in specialized subjects (Special Track)
5. Any program-specific documents (Annex 3)
6. ID: Thai National ID / 13-digit DMC card, or a valid passport for international applicants
7. A recent photo (within 6 months) in school uniform or formal attire, JPG/JPEG
8. Round 3 only: proof of a valid Non-Immigrant ED Visa, if applicable

Admitted students re-submit documents during new student registration, JUN 8 – 11, 2027 (https://stdinfo.reg.cmu.ac.th/undergraduate/index.php). The semester opens JUN 21, 2027.

## Fees

- Application fee: THB 1,200 per program (non-refundable)
- English Placement Test fee: THB 400 (non-refundable)
- Payment: QR code (no fee), credit card (~2.1% fee), Alipay or WeChat Pay (~1.7% fee)
- The international student fee of THB 20,000 per semester is already included in the tuition shown for each program.

## Academic qualifications and equivalency

Applicants must hold or be in the final year of Matthayom Suksa 6 (M.6) or an accredited equivalent. Equivalents (Annex 1) include: GCE A-Level (3 subjects, A*–E), IB Diploma (24+ points with EE/TOK/CAS), GED (from May 2017: at least 145 in each of 4 subjects; before May 2017: total 2,250 with at least 410 per subject), NCEA (New Zealand), international school Grade 12 / Year 13 diploma, Chinese high school system, Myanmar Matriculation (K-12 pass certificate and marks; or K-11 plus one year of undergraduate study or a 1-year diploma), and BTEC Level 3.

## After admission

- Purchase health insurance as required by CMU.
- International students request visa supporting documents through the CMU-IRD-VISA Online System (https://ird.cmu.ac.th/visa-online/). Documents are valid for 60 days from issuance — start the visa application immediately.
