# Software Engineering Aptitude Test — official format

## Who takes it

Only Software Engineering (SE) applicants on the Regular Track. It is taken in the 09:00 – 12:00 subject-test window together with the Mathematics exam.

## Structure: 30 multiple-choice questions + 1 essay

- **Part 1 (30 multiple-choice questions)**
  - Computer basics, software and hardware — 20 questions
  - Logical thinking — 10 questions
- **Part 2 (1 question)**
  - Demonstration by writing / essay — 1 question

Contact: interadmission-CAMT@cmu.ac.th, +66-53-920-299 ext. 107.

## How to use this knowledge base

- Computer basics, software and hardware: notes on hardware components, software and operating systems, data representation and number systems, networks and the internet, programming and software engineering basics, and security.
- Logical thinking: sequences, patterns, deduction, ordering puzzles, and simple algorithm tracing.
- Essay: a guide with likely prompt types, a structure, a timing plan and a scoring rubric.

CMU does not publish past papers for this test. The question styles here are realistic practice based on the published topic list, not leaked or official questions.

## Timing suggestion (3-hour window shared with Mathematics)

A reasonable split: about 75 minutes on Mathematics, 40 minutes on the 30 aptitude multiple-choice questions, 45 minutes on the essay, and about 20 minutes to review. Adjust after a timed practice run.
