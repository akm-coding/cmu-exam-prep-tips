# CMU English Placement Test (EPT)

## Who takes it

All applicants, in every program, who do not have a qualifying English proficiency score. It runs 13:00 – 16:00 on the written exam day. The fee is THB 400. Results are valid only for that admission round.

## Format (2 hours, 80 questions)

1. **Part 1 — Listening Comprehension (30 questions, 30 points):** short conversations (10), long conversations (10), short talks (10).
2. **Part 2 — Structure (30 questions, 30 points):** complete the sentences (10), error detection (10), fill-in-the-blank cloze passages (10).
3. **Part 3 — Reading Comprehension (20 questions, 20 points).**

Parts are taken in that order.

## Preparation tips

- Listening: practise with short dialogues and lecture clips; predict the question from the answer options before the audio plays; listen for the speaker's intention, not just keywords.
- Structure: review subject–verb agreement, tenses, articles, prepositions, comparatives, relative clauses, conditionals, parallel structure and word forms (noun/verb/adjective/adverb). Error detection questions often hide an agreement or word-form mistake.
- Cloze: read the whole passage first, then choose words that fit both grammar and meaning (connectors like however, therefore, although).
- Reading: skim for the main idea, scan for details, and watch for inference and vocabulary-in-context questions. Manage time: roughly 1–2 minutes per question.
